# v2 — Open Questions to Resolve Before Running the Phases

> Marked **(blocker)** = answer needed before that phase can start. **(soft)** = a sensible default can ship and be revisited later.

---

## Phase 1 — Inventory foundations

1. **(blocker) Lot number uniqueness scope.** Requirements say `lot_no UNIQUE`. Should that be globally unique across the whole DB, or unique only within `(fabric_id, color_id)`? Different suppliers can re-use the same lot number for different fabrics.
2. **(blocker) `purchase_price_egp` column on `rolls`.** Drop it entirely, or keep the column nullable so historical rolls retain their price (just stop writing it from Add Top)?
3. **(soft) Default unit for existing fabrics.** Migration defaults `unit = 'kg'` for all current fabrics. Are there any existing fabrics that should be `meter` from day one, or is kg correct for everything pre-v2?
4. **(soft) `length_m` for meter-fabric rolls — entered manually, or derived from weight + grams-per-meter?** Current assumption is pure manual entry. Some fabric shops use GSM × width to compute length from weight.
5. **(soft) Who can create lots?** Ziad in Add Top is obvious. Do you also want a standalone "Lots" admin page in Settings, or is Add Top + a list view enough?

## Phase 2 — Add Top UX

6. **(blocker) Lot numbers — auto-generated by the system (like `M-000001`) or always supplier-provided?** This changes the lot-create dialog: if supplier-provided, `lot_no` is a required text input; if auto, the user just optionally adds notes.
7. **(soft) Width default behaviour.** Current plan: width defaults to fabric's `width_cm` but is editable per roll. Weight: placeholder shows previous row's value but does NOT auto-fill (forces the user to confirm each one). Is that right, or do you want weight to also auto-fill like today?
8. **(soft) Can a single batch contain multiple fabrics?** Today it looks like one batch = one fabric. Confirm we keep that, or do you want multi-fabric batches?

## Phase 3 — Shipment per-fabric pricing

9. **(blocker) Does the per-fabric price entered at shipment receipt also update `FabricColorPrice.default_price_per_kg`?** Or is it strictly a one-shot per-shipment value that only writes `roll.selling_price_egp`?
10. **(blocker) Per-roll override on top of the per-fabric price — do you want that escape hatch, or strictly one price per fabric in the shipment with no overrides?** Requirements say "per fabric", which could be read either way.
11. **(soft) For meter-fabrics, if a roll has both `weight_kg` and `length_m`, confirm `length_m` always wins for the pricing math.**

## Phase 4 — Fulfillment destination (already gated)

12. **(blocker) `factory_direct` lifecycle** — the headline question from req 2.2. Do rolls skip the shop warehouse entirely and go straight to `sold`, or do they get a transient `factory_direct` warehouse state until handover is confirmed?
13. **(blocker) Where is the field set — at invoice creation in POS, or at shipment receipt as the requirements doc says?** The wording in the requirements is confusing because shipments and invoices aren't 1:1.
14. **(blocker) Is `fulfillment_destination` per invoice, or per line?** One customer could want some rolls now at the shop and some picked up at the factory.
15. **(soft) Who confirms factory handover, and through which UI?** Ahmed at the factory marks it done? Ziad at the shop marks it after the customer reports back? A QR scan at the factory?

## Phase 5 — POS price override + open-invoice deposit

16. **(blocker) `total_egp` on a no-lines deposit invoice — equal to the deposit, or 0?** Reports / cash drawer / customer credit will read differently for each. Current instinct: `total_egp = deposit, balance_egp = 0` initially, then `total_egp` recalculates when lines are added.
17. **(blocker) Refundable deposit?** If a customer pays a 500 EGP deposit and never returns, can the deposit be refunded later, or is it locked to that invoice forever?
18. **(soft) Does the per-unit override at POS update `FabricColorPrice.default_price_per_kg` for next time, or strictly one-time per sale?**

## Phase 6 — Return on scan

19. **(blocker) Refund payment method.** Mirror the original payment (cash back if paid in cash, bank if paid by bank), or always cash, or always credit-on-account?
20. **(blocker) Cross-branch returns.** Default plan is to block them. Do you want to allow them with manager approval instead?
21. **(soft) Refund amount when the roll was sold at an override price.** Refund the actual sold price (sticky), or the current `selling_price_egp` on the roll?
22. **(soft) Owner-approval threshold for returns.** Does v1.1 already have one (e.g., returns above 1000 EGP need Owner approval)? If yes, preserve. If no, do you want to add one now?

## Phase 7 — Payment methods (bank transfer + cheque)

23. **(blocker) Cheque due-date in the past — accept or reject?** A cheque dated yesterday is a red flag. Current plan only rejects `due_date < issue_date`.
24. **(soft) Cheque on printed receipt.** Should the cheque number + bank + due date appear on the printed invoice receipt, or only in the back-office cheque list?
25. **(soft) Bounced-cheque workflow** is explicitly a follow-up per the requirements doc. Confirm status management is entirely out of scope for v2.

## Phase 8 — Finance + business day

26. **(blocker) Stale invoice window — 7 calendar days or 7 business days under the new 10:30→00:00 definition?** Current assumption: 7 business days; the requirements doc isn't explicit.
27. **(blocker) Friday / Saturday weekend.** Does "business day" exclude Fridays in Egypt for any of the day-grouped reports, or are all 7 days of the week treated equally and the only window change is 10:30→00:00?
28. **(soft) Historical reports re-bucket.** Default plan: old rows render on the old calendar-day window; only new rows use the new business-day field. Confirm that's acceptable, or do you want a one-time backfill?

## Phase 9 — HR module

29. **(blocker) Advance repayment mechanic.** Do advances get repaid by:
    - (a) deduction from the next salary month (i.e., an advance auto-creates `hr_deductions` rows), or
    - (b) separate cash repayments tracked in `hr_advances.repaid_egp` and the salary is paid in full, or
    - (c) both options available?
    Current prompt assumes (b). The most common practice is (a).
30. **(blocker) Multiple disbursements per `(employee, month)`?** Current schema has a UNIQUE constraint preventing this. But bonuses, 13th salary, mid-month adjustments would need multiple rows. Do you want UNIQUE, or many-per-month with a `kind` field (`'salary' | 'bonus' | 'adjustment'`)?
31. **(blocker) Employee fields scope.** Current set: `name_ar`, `role_ar`, `base_salary_egp`, `is_active`. Do you also want: phone, national ID, hire date, termination date, branch_id? The requirements doc is minimal so the prompt stayed minimal.
32. **(soft) Permissions matrix.** Current plan invents `hr.view`, `hr.manage`, `hr.salary.disburse`, `hr.advance.create`, `hr.deduction.create`. Do you want simpler (`hr.view` + `hr.manage` only)?
